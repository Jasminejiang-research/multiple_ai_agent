# Consulting Memo Template

## Purpose

Use this decision-oriented memo to turn a defined business question into a
clear recommendation and action plan. Replace bracketed instructions with
case-specific content. Cite material facts with `source_id`; label judgments
and unverified inputs as assumptions.

## Memo Header

- **To:** `[Decision maker or steering group]`
- **From:** `[Author or project team]`
- **Date:** `[YYYY-MM-DD]`
- **Subject:** `[Decision to be made]`
- **Decision deadline:** `[Date, if applicable]`

## Executive Recommendation

State the recommended action in one or two sentences, followed by:

- expected business outcome;
- investment or resources required;
- critical condition that must be true;
- immediate decision requested.

## Situation and Decision Context

Define the current state, why action is needed now, the decision scope, and
important constraints. Distinguish facts from stakeholder opinions.

## Key Question

`How should [organization] achieve [outcome] for [customer/market] within
[time and constraints]?`

## Findings

Organize findings as an answer-first argument. Each finding should contain:

1. a concise conclusion;
2. the evidence and `source_id`;
3. the implication for the decision;
4. the confidence level and remaining uncertainty.

are accepted, which alternatives were rejected, and what evidence could

## Financial and Operational Implications

State estimated one-time costs, recurring costs, benefits, capacity changes,
and cash-flow timing. Provide formulas, ranges, and sensitivity cases rather
than unsupported precision.

## Final Review Checklist

- The requested decision is explicit.
- The recommendation follows from the findings and comparison criteria.
- Material facts are traceable to sources.
- Assumptions, uncertainties, and dissenting evidence are visible.
- Actions have owners, timing, and measurable outcomes.
