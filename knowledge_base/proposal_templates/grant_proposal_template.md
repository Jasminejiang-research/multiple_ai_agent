# Grant Proposal Template

## Purpose

Use this template to prepare a grant proposal that connects a documented need
to measurable outcomes, feasible activities, and an accountable budget.
Always adapt the structure to the funder's published instructions. Never
invent beneficiaries, partnerships, prior results, or eligibility evidence.

## Cover Information

- **Project title:** `[Concise, outcome-focused title]`
- **Applicant:** `[Legal organization name]`
- **Funding program:** `[Program and call identifier]`
- **Project period:** `[Start and end dates]`
- **Amount requested:** `[Currency and amount]`
- **Primary contact:** `[Name, role, contact details]`

## 1. Executive Summary

Summarize the need, target population, proposed intervention, expected
outcomes, applicant capability, requested amount, and project duration.

## 2. Organizational Background

Describe the applicant's mission, legal status, relevant experience,
governance, team capacity, prior verified results, and fit with the funding
program. Attach evidence for credentials and track record.

## 3. Statement of Need

Define the affected population, geography, scale, causes, and consequences of
the problem. Explain why existing services are insufficient and why the
project is timely.

**Evidence required:** recent, credible sources and, when available,
community-level data. Cite each material claim with `source_id`.

## 5. Project Design and Activities

`Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Short-term outcomes → Long-term impact`

For each link, document the causal assumption and evidence supporting it.

## 9. Equity, Safeguarding, and Ethics

Address meaningful participation, inclusion, informed consent, data
protection, safeguarding, unintended harm, grievance mechanisms, and
regulatory obligations applicable to the project.

## 10. Team, Governance, and Partnerships

Define responsibilities, decision rights, financial oversight, partner
commitments, and conflict-resolution procedures. Mark proposed partnerships
as unconfirmed until documentary evidence exists.

## 13. Sustainability and Scale

Explain which benefits or services continue after the grant, the future
funding model, capacity transfer, stakeholder ownership, and responsible
scale or exit criteria.

## Appendices and Sources

Include only requested supporting documents. List sources as:

`[source_id] — [title], [publisher], [date], [URL or file name]`

## Final Review Checklist

- Every requirement in the funder's call is addressed.
- Need, activities, outputs, outcomes, and budget align.
- Objectives are measurable and have realistic baselines.
- Claims, prior results, eligibility, and partnerships are verifiable.
- Budget calculations reconcile and contain only eligible costs.
- Ethics, safeguarding, evaluation, risks, and sustainability are explicit.
