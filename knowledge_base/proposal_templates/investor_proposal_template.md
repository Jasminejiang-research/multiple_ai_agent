# Investor Proposal Template

## Purpose

Use this template to prepare an evidence-aware proposal for prospective
investors. Replace every bracketed instruction with project-specific content.
Label estimates as assumptions, and attach a `source_id` to every material
market, competitor, trend, or benchmark claim.

## Proposal Title

`[Company or product name] — [one-sentence investment thesis]`

## 1. Executive Summary

- Company and product: `[What is being built, and for whom?]`
- Problem: `[What costly or urgent problem exists?]`
- Solution: `[How does the product solve it?]`
- Business model: `[Who pays, how, and how often?]`
- Traction or current stage: `[Verified result or explicitly labeled assumption]`
- Funding request and use: `[Amount, runway, and intended outcomes]`

## 2. Problem

Describe the customer problem, its frequency, current consequences, and the
inadequacy of existing alternatives. Separate observed evidence from the
team's hypotheses.

**Evidence required:** customer research, operational data, or credible
third-party research.

## 3. Target Customer

Define the primary segment, buyer, user, use case, purchasing trigger, and
adoption barriers. Avoid broad labels such as "all small businesses."

## 4. Market Opportunity

Present TAM, SAM, and SOM with the calculation method, base year, geography,
and assumptions. Prefer a bottom-up estimate and reconcile it with credible
top-down sources.

| Market layer | Definition | Formula | Estimate | Source IDs / assumptions |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| TAM | `[Full relevant demand]` | `[Formula]` | `[Value]` | `[source_id or assumption]` |
| SAM | `[Serviceable segment]` | `[Formula]` | `[Value]` | `[source_id or assumption]` |
| SOM | `[Realistic initial share]` | `[Formula]` | `[Value]` | `[source_id or assumption]` |

## 5. Solution

Explain the product workflow, core capabilities, customer outcome, current
development status, and what is deliberately out of scope.

## 6. Value Proposition

State the measurable customer value and why it is meaningfully better than the
status quo. Connect each claimed benefit to evidence or a testable hypothesis.

## 7. Competitor Analysis

Compare direct competitors, indirect alternatives, and doing nothing. Do not
claim a competitor lacks a feature without current evidence.

| Alternative | Target segment | Strength | Limitation | Differentiation | Source IDs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[Name]` | `[Segment]` | `[Strength]` | `[Verified limitation]` | `[Difference]` | `[source_id]` |

## 8. Business Model

Describe the payer, pricing unit, revenue streams, sales cycle, retention
logic, and expected gross-margin profile. Mark unvalidated pricing as an
assumption.

## 9. Go-to-Market Strategy

Identify the beachhead segment, acquisition channels, sales motion,
partnerships, launch sequence, and metrics used to validate each stage.

## 10. Financial Assumptions

Show the model period, currency, revenue drivers, cost drivers, headcount,
cash requirements, and unit economics. Use formulas and ranges where
uncertainty is high; do not present projections as guaranteed outcomes.

| Assumption | Base case | Downside | Upside | Basis |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `[Driver]` | `[Value]` | `[Value]` | `[Value]` | `[source_id or assumption]` |

## 11. Risks and Mitigations

Cover market, product, execution, regulatory, financial, and evidence risks.
For each risk, state likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner, and early-warning
indicator.

## 12. Implementation Roadmap

| Milestone | Target period | Deliverable | Success metric | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[Milestone]` | `[Date/range]` | `[Output]` | `[Measure]` | `[Dependency]` |

## 13. Appendix

Include supporting calculations, source notes, interview summaries, glossary,
and unresolved assumptions. List sources in a traceable format:

`[source_id] — [title], [publisher], [date], [URL or file name]`

## Final Review Checklist

- All 13 sections are present and internally consistent.
- Key claims have source IDs or are explicitly labeled assumptions.
- Market and financial calculations can be reproduced.
- Competitor information is current and fairly represented.
- Funding use links to milestones and measurable outcomes.
- Material uncertainties and risks are visible to the reader.
